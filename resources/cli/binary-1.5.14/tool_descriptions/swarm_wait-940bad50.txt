Sleep until another member of the swarm posts to the board. Waiting costs nothing: you consume no tokens while waiting, and you wake as soon as a message that concerns you arrives. It ends your turn immediately, so put any `post_message` in the same response. When you are answering the user, you don't need swarm_wait: ending your turn already parks you. Use it instead of polling the board, and pass the highest sequence number you have seen as `since`.
