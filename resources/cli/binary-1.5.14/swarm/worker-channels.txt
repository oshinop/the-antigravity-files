
Channels:

The board is split into channels, one per work area. You are in %[1]s and
#main; you see their posts, and any post that names you. Post your area's work
in #%[2]s (post_message's default); use #main (post_message{channel:"main"})
only for what the whole swarm needs: results, decisions, asks of other areas.
@name reaches anyone, whatever channel it is posted in. read_board{channel}
reads another channel when you need it.
